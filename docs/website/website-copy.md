## 2. How it works

> ### it's just three seconds of your life
>
> **1. tap.** one button. no setup, no account.
> **2. talk.** say whatever's on your mind.
> **3. done.** fil writes it down and gives it a short title, so you'll find it later.

`[Visual: three simple frames or a single continuous animation. Keep it literal — this is the whole

## 3. The turn — what fil isn't

> ### some thoughts are just thoughts
>
> fil is not a productivity app. not a second brain. not a journal, a task manager, or a system to
> keep up with.
>
> no inbox. no tags. no folders. no streaks. no due dates. no dashboards.
>
> optimization culture says your thoughts only count if they're organized, tagged, and turned into
> action. we don't think that's true. some thoughts just need to exist.
>
> fil has zero opinion on how you should organize yourself. that's the point.

`[Visual: lots of negative space. This is the emotional core of the page — let it breathe. Maybe the
long "no ___" list rendered quiet and small.]`

## 4. What's inside (gently)

> ### everything you need, nothing you don't
>
> **talk, don't type.** your voice becomes a titled note in one tap.
>
> **titled on your device.** apple's on-device intelligence writes a short, natural title — so a
> week later you find "call the landlord," not a wall of text.
>
> **yours, and only yours.** no account, no ads, no tracking. your fils live on your phone.
>
> **capture from anywhere.** a home screen widget, live activity, and a share sheet to send a link,
> photo, or note straight into fil.
>
> **a place, not a pile.** a calm, considered design — with a little sound, ambient screensavers,
> and gradients that make your notes feel like somewhere to be.

`[Visual: a soft grid of gradient blob cards — real fils. Let the color come entirely from the
blobs; keep the rest near-monochrome.]`

## 5. Privacy

> ### it stays with you
>
> fil was built local-first. your recordings, transcripts, and notes live on your device — not on
> our servers. there's no account to make and nothing to log into.
>
> titles are generated on-device. transcription runs on-device wherever your device supports it.
> no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no profile of you.

## Notes for build
- **CTA:** the App Store badge is the only real call to action; repeat it after the hero and again
  near the footer. Everything else is "read more" at most.
- **Privacy claim guardrail:** don't write absolute "100% on-device" / "nothing ever leaves your
  phone" for *transcription* — the honest phrasing is "on your device" for titling and
  "on-device wherever your device supports it" for transcription (matches the Privacy Policy). App
  titling *is* fully on-device and can be stated plainly.
- **Type:** DM Sans for everything, DM Mono for small labels/metadata if used. Lowercase headings.
- **Color:** near-monochrome canvas; let one or two blob gradients carry all the color.
- **Motion:** slow, organic, calm. No scroll-jacking.
- **/press:** link to the press-kit page (to be drafted next) — press-kit contents live in the
  growth plan (audit P1: icon, framed + raw screenshots, copy, fact sheet, founder bio, promo video).
