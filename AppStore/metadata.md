## Description
```
some thoughts are just thoughts.

fil is a quiet place to speak your mind and let it be. tap once, say what's on your mind, and fil
turns it into a short, titled note you can actually find later — all on your device.

that's it. that's the app.

fil is not a productivity app, a second brain, a journal, or a system to keep up with. there's no
inbox, no tags, no folders, no streaks, no due dates. it has zero opinion on how you should
organize yourself. some thoughts just need to exist.

— talk, don't type. your voice becomes a titled note.
— titles are written on your device by apple's on-device intelligence.
— no account. no ads. no tracking. your fils stay on your phone.
— capture from anywhere: a home screen widget, live activity, and a share sheet to send a link,
  image, or text straight into fil.
— a calm, considered design, with optional sound and ambient screensavers.

speak your mind. let it be.

questions or ideas? mason@garcera.us
```
