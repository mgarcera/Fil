# Fil — Screenshot Spec

**Required:** at least one **6.9″ iPhone** set (also serves 6.7″). Capture on an iPhone 16 Pro Max
/ 15 Pro Max in portrait. Put caption text in the top/bottom safe zone — Apple's OCR indexes
screenshot text, so it adds ASO. Voice = the manifesto: lowercase, calm, no hype.

| # | Caption (lowercase) | Frame |
|---|---------------------|-------|
| 1 | speak your mind. | composer mid-capture over the ambient gradient (hero) |
| 2 | your words become a titled note. | the blob grid with several auto-titled fils |
| 3 | titled on your device. | a fil open in the article view, title highlighted |
| 4 | one glance away. | home screen with the pinned widget + Dynamic Island composited |
| 5 | no inbox. no tags. no system. | a clean grid, or the "from mason" card |
| 6 | let thoughts be. | a screensaver / ambient moment |

**Optional but recommended:** a 15–30s App Preview video — *tap → speak → titled note* — which
lifts product-page conversion.

## Notes
- Keep the device frame consistent across all six.
- Don't claim "100% on-device" for transcription anywhere in the shots until on-device speech is
  guaranteed on all target devices (see audit #6 / the Privacy Policy wording). "on your device"
  as used for *titling* in shot 3 is accurate today.
- Reuse the app's real gradients — the visual identity is a selling point.
